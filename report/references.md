## C. How some entries may be used (review answer 39)

| Entry | Supports | Does not support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Yamins & DiCarlo (2016) | Task-trained networks as models of ventral-stream object responses | CLIP or DINOv2 as the front end of action observation |
| Heyes (2001); Cook et al. (2014); Catmur et al. (2007) | Learned visuomotor associations | Training on full-body views only |
| Tsakiris & Haggard (2005) | Body-related constraints on self-attribution in the rubber hand illusion | Wrist continuity specifically (cite Tieri et al., 2015; Perez-Marcos et al., 2012) |
| Fourcaud & Brunel (2002) | The analytic firing-rate theory behind the twin's rate function | The simulated table the twin actually uses (say so) |
| Horowitz & Manski (2000) | Bounds under missing data | The two treatments of over-driven readouts, which are a sensitivity analysis, not bounds (finding F8) |
| Devanne et al. (1997) | The sigmoid input–output curve of the corticospinal pathway | A specific gain factor (the "4–7×" figure is not verifiable; do not quote it) |
